Support ViTrainer

ViTrainer is and will always remain free: no ads, no microtransactions and no pay-to-win mechanics. If you enjoy the game and wish to support server hosting costs, you can make a voluntary donation. All funds go directly toward domain and server operations.

Donations grant zero in-game advantages: every trainer competes on equal footing.
