ViTrainer guide

ViTrainer is a free Pokémon survival game in the style of Survivor.io and Mystery Dungeon that you play in your browser: pick a partner, roam an endless dungeon and your moves fire on their own while hordes of wild Pokémon try to surround you. How long can you last?

How to play

  • On PC: move with WASD or arrow keys. Switch moves with 1–4, Q/E or by clicking their icon. Use orbs with 5–8. Pause with Esc.
  • On phone or tablet (landscape): drag your finger anywhere to move, tap a move to switch to it, an orb to use it and the top-right button to pause.
  • With a controller: stick or D-pad to move, LB/RB to switch moves, X to use an orb and Y to pick another, Start to pause.
  • Your Pokémon attacks on its own with the active move. Your job is to dodge and position yourself well.
  • Collect experience to level up and pick one of three cards: new moves, upgrades to the ones you have, or boosts for your Pokémon.

Features

  • 1023 Pokémon from all 9 regions (see Pokédex), each with its own nature and ability impacting combat.
  • Physical and Special moves: accurately calculated based on Attack / Special Attack vs Defense / Special Defense.
  • Moves evolve in 3 tiers: reach max level with compatible upgrades to unlock devastating evolutions. Defeat rift bosses to unlock Bonds fusing up to 4 moves into one slot.
  • Smart enemies: enemies heal, shield, summon swarms, and telegraph their heavy attacks.
  • Boss battles, 18 Elemental Rift Arenas and the final Judgment of Arceus at minute 30.
  • Weather and Type Chart: Rain powers up Water and weakens Fire; sunshine, sandstorm, snow and fog.
  • Shiny Pokémon in the wild (1 in 4096), in rifts and in the gacha.
  • Regional slot machine gacha: pull 10 or 100 with earned tickets. Complete a regional Pokédex for the Shiny Charm.
  • Kecleon Shop: Mints, Ability Capsules and 8 new TMs daily.
  • Held items like Leftovers, Choice Band, Focus Sash, Life Orb and tactical single-use Orbs.
  • Online Co-op up to 8 players: share XP and revive fallen teammates.
  • Daily quests, 141 achievements with medals, titles and global rankings.

Survival tips

  1. Never stand still: move in wide circles so the hordes follow in a line behind you.
  2. Leverage the type chart: switch to super effective moves against approaching waves.
  3. Telegraphed markers on the ground indicate imminent attacks: step out immediately.
  4. Prioritize taking down support enemies that heal or shield other Pokémon.
  5. Save Slumber Orbs or Blowback Orbs for moments when you get cornered.
  6. Spend coins upgrading your favorite partner and check the Kecleon Shop for synergistic TMs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ViTrainer free?

Yes, completely. It's a non-profit fan game with no ads or purchases. If you like, you can support it with a voluntary donation, which only pays for the servers and gives no advantages.

Do I need to download anything?

No. You play in your browser on PC, tablet or phone. If you like, you can install it as an app with “Add to Home Screen”.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, in landscape mode. Put your finger anywhere on the screen and drag to move, tap a move to switch to it, an orb to use it and the top-right button to pause. In Settings > Controls you can place the buttons wherever you like.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes: any Xbox, PlayStation or Switch controller connected to your PC. Stick or D-pad to move, LB / RB to switch moves, X to use an orb and Y to pick another one, and Start to pause.

Which languages is it in?

Two official languages: Vietnamese and English. It uses your browser language and you can change it anytime in Settings.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes: online co-op supports up to 8 players. Add your friends by nickname, invite them to your room and start the game. Experience is shared and you can revive a fallen teammate by standing next to them for 3 seconds.

How many Pokémon are there?

1023 Pokémon from all 9 regions (Kanto to Paldea), all playable as your partner, with their shiny form when one exists.

How do I get more Pokémon?

From the gacha, which uses tickets you earn by playing: regular Pokémon drop tickets now and then, and bosses and rift legendaries sometimes drop ×10 tickets. Each region has its own banner and completing a region's Pokédex awards its Shiny Charm. Tickets can't be bought with real money.

What is in the Kecleon Shop?

You buy with PokéCoins you earn by playing: Mints (change nature), Ability Capsules (change ability) and 8 new TMs every day, which permanently teach a move outside its types. Purchases go to your Bag.

What are rifts?

Mysterious portals that open during runs. If you enter, you battle a legendary Pokémon in its elemental arena (18 arenas). Win to receive rewards and unlock move bonding.

Is my progress saved?

With an account (username and password, or Google) your game is saved in the cloud and you can continue on any device. Guests can play right away, but nothing is saved permanently.

Is this an official Pokémon game?

No. It's a free, non-profit fan project, unrelated to Nintendo, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.
