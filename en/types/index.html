Weather Effects

  • Rain: Water boosted (+50%), Fire weakened (-50%).
  • Harsh Sunlight: Fire boosted (+50%), Water weakened (-50%).
  • Sandstorm: Rock, Ground and Steel buffed; others take passive damage.
  • Snowfall: Ice buffed; slows down targets.
  • Fog: Ghost, Fairy and Psychic boosted.
